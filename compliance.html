Accessibility Statement

Effective October 30, 2026

Tirion is committed to making our website and applications usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we do

  • Use semantic page structure, headings, and landmarks for screen readers;
  • Support full keyboard navigation with clearly visible focus indicators;
  • Design for strong color contrast between text and backgrounds;
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • Respect your device's "reduce motion" preference, and offer a Pause background motion control at the bottom of every page;
  • Build layouts that adapt to screen sizes and browser zoom.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review our pages as we add features and welcome feedback that helps us improve.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier or need information in an alternative format, contact us at [accessibility contact email]. Please describe the page and the issue, and we will respond as quickly as we can.

Cookie Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Tirion uses cookies and similar technologies.

This website

Our marketing website does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It stores a single preference in your browser's local storage, your choice to pause or play the background animation, so the site remembers it on your next visit. This preference never leaves your device.

The Tirion applications

When you sign in, the applications use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, maintain your session securely, and protect against unauthorized access. These cookies are required for the applications to work and cannot be switched off in our systems.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you may not be able to sign in to the applications. You can clear the motion preference at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If we introduce new cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.
